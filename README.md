# Pyramid of Capitalist System — A Trickle-Down Arcade

> *After the iconic 1911 Industrial Workers of the World (I.W.W.) poster by Nedeljkovich, Brashich & Kuharich (Cleveland, Ohio).*  
> *Rendered in historic lithograph print aesthetic with layered paper grain.*

🎮 **[Play Live on GitHub Pages](https://21e8-miner.github.io/pyramid-of-capitalism/)**

## 📖 About

The money bag at the pinnacle pours out wealth, but every tier skims its heavy cut:
1. **$ CAPITALISM $**: The money bag atop the pyramid.
2. **WE RULE YOU (Nous vous gouvernons)**: The monarchs and presidents in velvet coats.
3. **WE FOOL YOU (Nous vous trompons)**: The clergy and philosophers in flowing robes.
4. **WE SHOOT AT YOU (Nous vous fusillons)**: The militia with rifles and artillery.
5. **WE EAT FOR YOU (Nous mangeons pour vous)**: The banquet of aristocrats.
6. **WE WORK FOR ALL · WE FEED ALL (Nous travaillons pour vous tous · Nous vous nourrissons tous)**: The workers holding up the entire foundation.

## 🕹️ Controls (Mobile & Desktop)

- **Touch & Drag / Mouse**: Move the worker and cap left/right to catch falling coins and bread scraps.
- **Dodge Musket Fire**: Avoid gunfire from the *WE SHOOT AT YOU* tier!
- **Solidarity Meter & STRIKE (GRÈVE)**:
  - Catching coins and scraps charges your red **SOLIDARITY** banner.
  - When full (100%), **TAP ANYWHERE / PRESS SPACE** to heave and send a tier tumbling down!
  - Topple all tiers to bring down the money bag itself!
- **Left / Right Arrow / A & D (Desktop)**: Move left / right.
- **🔊 Sound Toggle / M Key**: Toggle retro chimes and synthesizer audio.

---

## 📱 Mobile-First Features

- **Direct Touch Panning**: Responsive one-finger slide tracking across the base of the pyramid.
- **Haptic Feedback**: Vibrations on coin catches, damage taken, and tier-toppling strikes.
- **Portrait 720×1080 Viewport**: Full aspect scaling on all mobile screens with notch and safe-area padding.
- **Persistent High Scores**: Local storage tracking your highest centimes banked.
